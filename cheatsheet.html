Cheat sheet

What to text BidBuddy for a roofing estimate

  • Total roof squares
  • Roof type and shingle or material selection
  • Tear-off layers
  • Pitch
  • One-story or two-story
  • Ridge, eave, valley, chimney, skylight, and penetration details
  • Decking or repair notes
  • Anything unusual about access or cleanup
Example:

28 squares, architectural shingles, one layer tear-off, 6/12 pitch, two-story, 58 ft ridge, 42 ft valley, 2 pipe boots, one chimney, include synthetic underlayment.
