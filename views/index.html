How First-Time Onboarding Works

When a brand-new phone number texts your Twilio number for the very first time, the bot automatically sends a welcome packet explaining how it works (defined in firstTimeOnboarding on the Prompts tab). After that, every text is treated as a job request.

✅ If their first text already contains a job (like "1200 sqft LVP install"), the bot sends the onboarding message AND processes the job — back-to-back.
