How it works

Three steps. No app to download, no dashboard to learn.

1

Load your rates once

Enter your labor rates, material costs, markup, minimums, and service radius. Takes about ten minutes and you only do it once.

2

Text the job

Send the details the way you'd say them out loud — room size, material, tear-out, prep. Plain language is fine.

3

Get a priced estimate

BidBuddy runs it against your numbers and texts back a breakdown you can send straight to the customer.

Built for the trades

Starting with flooring, expanding across contractor verticals.

Flooring

LVP, tile, carpet, and hardwood. Demo, subfloor prep, install, and trim priced per square foot from your own rate sheet.

Roofing

Tear-off, install, and decking priced per square. Shingle, metal, and underlayment costs configured by you.

More trades coming

The pricing engine isn't hardcoded to one trade. New verticals get added as contractors ask for them.

Why text?

Because it's what contractors already use. Nobody wants another login, another dashboard, or another subscription to forget about. If you can send a text from a jobsite, you can send an estimate from a jobsite.
