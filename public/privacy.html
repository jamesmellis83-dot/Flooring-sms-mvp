Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8/08/2026

This policy explains what BidBuddy USA LLC ("BidBuddy," "we," "us") collects from users of bidbuddyusa.com and our SMS service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact information — business name, contact name, email address, and mobile phone number you provide during onboarding.
  • Pricing and business data — labor rates, material costs, markup, minimums, and service radius you enter to configure your estimates.
  • Message content — the text messages you exchange with our service, used to generate and deliver estimates.
  • Technical data — standard log information such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps.

How we use it

  • To generate job estimates using the pricing you configured.
  • To send and receive text messages you have consented to receive.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
SMS data: We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used solely to operate the BidBuddy SMS service.

Service providers

We share limited information with vendors who help us operate the service, including our SMS carrier and messaging provider, our cloud hosting provider, and our AI language model provider used to interpret job details and generate estimates. These providers may process your data only to perform services for us.

Data retention

We keep your account and pricing information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us at [SUPPORT EMAIL], and we will delete your information except where we are required to retain it by law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts.
  • Contact us to access, correct, or delete your information.
  • Close your account at any time.

Children

The service is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
