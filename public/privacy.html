Privacy Policy

BidBuddy USA LLC collects information needed to provide contractor estimating, onboarding, account support, and SMS communication services.

Information collected

  • Business contact information such as name, company, email address, and phone number.
  • Contractor pricing inputs, service area details, and estimate-related job information you provide.
  • SMS messages and support communications sent to or from BidBuddy USA.

How information is used

Information is used to configure contractor pricing, respond to estimate requests, provide support, improve the service, and communicate about your account.

SMS data

SMS consent and phone numbers are used to provide BidBuddy messaging services. BidBuddy USA does not sell SMS consent information.

Data sharing

BidBuddy USA may use service providers to operate hosting, messaging, communications, and support workflows. Information may also be shared when required by law or to protect the service.
