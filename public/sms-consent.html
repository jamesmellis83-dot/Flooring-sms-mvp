Last updated: 8/08/2026

Summary: BidBuddy USA sends text messages only to contractors who have opted in. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Program description

BidBuddy USA operates an SMS program that allows contractors to request and receive job estimates, project updates, appointment information, customer support, and account notifications by text message.

How you opt in

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box on our contractor onboarding form at bidbuddyusa.com/onboarding and submitting your mobile number. Consent is collected at the point where you provide your number, and the checkbox is not pre-selected.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

After you opt in, you will receive a confirmation message:

BidBuddy: You're subscribed to estimate and account texts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on how often you request estimates or how many active jobs you have. Messages are conversational and are generally sent in response to your activity.

Cost

BidBuddy USA does not charge for text messages. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. You will receive one final confirmation message and will not receive further messages unless you opt in again.

BidBuddy: You're unsubscribed and won't receive further messages. Reply START to opt back in.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance.

BidBuddy: Estimating help by text. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel. Support: [SUPPORT EMAIL]

You can also reach us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] or [SUPPORT PHONE].

Supported carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission from your wireless service provider.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
